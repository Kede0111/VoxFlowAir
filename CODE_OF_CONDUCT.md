# Code of Conduct

VoxFlowAir follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback
gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people using and building VoxFlowAir. Harassment, insults,
personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable,
in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub
(for example by opening a private security advisory on this repository if no other private channel is
listed on the maintainer's profile). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove
comments, commits or contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or
seriously break it.
